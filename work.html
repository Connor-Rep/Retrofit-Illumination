Every set of lights gets torn all the way down before we touch a wire. Here's what's come through the shop, and the kind of work we take on.

RX-8 Build

Dual Halo Ring Retrofit

Both headlights came apart down to the housing: lens desealed, reflector out, factory harness mapped before anything got cut. A halo ring went in around each projector, wired clean off the low-beam circuit so there's no separate switch or extra wiring hanging around under the hood.

Before either housing went back together for good, it sat wired to a spare battery on the garage floor for a full test run, checking the rings stayed even, no flicker, no dead spots, before committing to the reseal.

Kit Build

Daily Driver Underglow Kit

Not every job is a full teardown. This one was a straightforward kit build: four RGB bars wired to steel subframe brackets instead of the flimsy plastic clips most kits ship with, harness routed and labeled to stay clear of the exhaust heat shield, and set to switch off automatically above a set speed before it shipped out.
