Do you install what you sell?

No, we build and ship the kit; fitting it is on you or your installer. Everything ships with instructions written for someone doing this in their driveway, not a shop bay.

Do you do star ceiling / roof headliners?

No, that's the one thing we're not offering right now. Everything else on this page, we do.

Can I get a kit sized for a specific vehicle?

That's how we sell underglow: send us your year/make/model on the quote form and we'll size the bars and wiring harness to match your car before it ships.

What's the warranty?

12 months on any kit we sell, parts and workmanship, as long as it hasn't been opened up or rewired by someone else.
