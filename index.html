How It Works

Four steps, no shortcuts.

  • 01

    Teardown

    Housing comes fully apart — lens desealed, reflector pulled, factory wiring mapped before anything gets cut.

  • 02

    Mod & Fit

    Halo rings, sequential boards, or mirror film get mounted, wired, and dry-fit against the lens for beam pattern and shadowing.

  • 03

    Reseal

    Housing goes back together with fresh butyl or urethane sealant and oven-cured — same process the factory used, done by hand.

  • 04

    Bench Test

    Wired up and run before it's ever bolted back on — checked for even output and no dead spots before the reseal is trusted.
